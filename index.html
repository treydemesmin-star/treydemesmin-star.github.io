01 / Professional self-assessment

My development as a computer scientist

Initial reflection · Final capstone assessment planned for Module Seven

I am completing my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Southern New Hampshire University. My goal is to begin a career in software quality assurance or software engineering, with a particular interest in aerospace and defense. Through Java, C++, and Python projects, I have learned to connect requirements, implementation, and testing rather than judge a program only by whether it runs.

My coursework has developed my understanding of modular design, data structures, database applications, and clear technical communication. CS 320 helped me use automated tests to check expected behavior and invalid inputs. CS 300 strengthened my ability to compare data structures and explain performance tradeoffs. CS 340 connected data access and an interactive dashboard in one application.

Read my reflection on collaboration, security, and career growth

Agile coursework in CS 250 helped me understand how feedback, shared requirements, and incremental work support a team. In this capstone, I will use review questions and a feedback log to document how instructor input influences my decisions. I want technical explanations to be useful to developers while keeping the purpose of each change clear to other stakeholders.

Security and reliability belong in the design process. My planned enhancements include validation, controlled data access, protection of credentials, and tests for unsuccessful operations. My event tracking application in CS 360 also gave me experience thinking about the relationship between user interfaces and application behavior.

My Air National Guard experience reinforces the importance I place on accountability and following procedures. The three projects below will provide academic evidence of my technical growth. As I complete the enhancements, I will update this assessment with actual results, challenges, and lessons learned.
